SAMARA CARE ERP 1.3.1 — QUICK HEALTH WHATSAPP REPORT

Upload and replace only:
- app.js
- index.html
- service-worker.js

No Supabase SQL is required.

How it works:
1. Admin/Manager opens Intelligent Reports.
2. Selects a patient and report date.
3. Generates the Patient Report.
4. Clicks WhatsApp.
5. Selects Quick Health Update.
6. The ERP automatically creates and previews a concise report using the actual selected-date records:
   - Current clinical status
   - Latest available vital signs
   - Medicine administrations and exceptions
   - Daily-care completion
   - Food/intake records
   - Physiotherapy
   - Incidents
7. Choose English or Tamil.
8. Click Open WhatsApp, review and send.

The quick report does not invent missing values. When a section has no record, it states that no entry was recorded.
WhatsApp access remains restricted to Admin and Manager.
