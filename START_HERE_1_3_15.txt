SAMARA CARE ERP 1.3.15 — PATIENT DISCHARGE & AUTOMATIC BED RELEASE

1. Run once in Supabase SQL Editor:
   40_patient_discharge_auto_bed_release.sql

2. Upload and replace only:
   app.js
   index.html
   service-worker.js

Workflow:
- Nurse/Admin/Manager can initiate discharge.
- Nurse completes clinical details and handover checklist.
- Accounts marks billing clearance.
- Admin/Manager verifies all clearances and completes discharge.
- Caregiver/Kitchen can view only.

Completion automatically:
- Marks patient inactive/discharged.
- Saves discharge date and time.
- Removes room and bed from patient record.
- Changes Room/Bed status to Available.
- Stops active medicines, care plans and physiotherapy plans.
- Completes active Special Nurse assignments.
- Records completion in Audit Trail.

Mandatory before completion:
- Billing clearance
- Clinical clearance
- Room/property clearance
- Medicines handed over
- Discharge summary handed over
