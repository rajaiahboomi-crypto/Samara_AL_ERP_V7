SAMARA CARE ERP 1.3.42 — RETURN TO SHIFT TASKS AFTER SAVE

Upload:
- app.js
- index.html
- service-worker.js
- styles-1.3.42.css

No Supabase SQL is required.

When Daily Care, Medication Administration or Physiotherapy is opened from Shift Tasks:
- The regular clinical form opens with the relevant task preselected.
- After a successful save, confirmation appears briefly.
- The ERP automatically returns to Shift Tasks.
- The worklist refreshes and the completed item no longer remains pending.

When these modules are opened directly from the left menu, the page remains open after saving.
