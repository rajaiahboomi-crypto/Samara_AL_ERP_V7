SAMARA CARE ERP 1.3.16 — BILLS & CHARGES WORKFLOW

1. Run once in Supabase SQL Editor:
   41_bills_charges_workflow.sql

2. Upload and replace only:
   app.js
   index.html
   service-worker.js

Access:
- Nurse: raise and edit a bill/charge query.
- Manager: raise, review, enter final fees, approve and post.
- Accounts: raise, review, enter final fees, approve and post.
- Admin: full access.
- Caregiver/Kitchen: no access.

Typical categories:
- Doctor Visit
- Medicines
- Physiotherapy
- Additional Food
- Diagnostic Test
- Consumables
- Medical Equipment
- Ambulance / Transport
- Special Nurse
- Procedure / Dressing
- Other

Workflow:
1. Nurse raises the request.
2. If bill/receipt and amount are available, the nurse enters them.
3. If unavailable, the nurse may leave the final fee blank.
4. Accounts or Manager verifies the bill/provider and enters the final charge.
5. Approve & Post transfers the charge automatically to Billing & Payments / Patient Ledger.
6. Duplicate posting is prevented.
7. Green/red confirmation pop-ups and Audit Trail recording are included.
