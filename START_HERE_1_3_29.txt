SAMARA CARE ERP 1.3.29 — CURRENT & UPCOMING MEDICINES ONLY

Upload and replace only:
- app.js
- index.html
- service-worker.js

No Supabase SQL is required.

Corrected:
- Patient Edit now displays only active medicines that are current or scheduled for the future.
- Expired, completed and inactive medicines are hidden.
- Exact duplicate medicine entries are removed from the displayed list.
- Medicines are arranged by start date and medicine name.
- The section heading is changed to “Current and Upcoming Medicines”.

History protection:
- Saving Patient Edit no longer permanently deletes old prescription orders.
- Existing active orders are archived as inactive.
- The newly verified current/upcoming list is saved as active.
- Previous prescriptions remain preserved as historical records.

The Patient File medicine tab also shows only the current/upcoming active medicines.
