SAMARA CARE ERP 1.3.3 — FUTURE REPORT DATE BLOCK & PATIENT UPDATE POP-UP

Upload and replace only:
- app.js
- index.html
- service-worker.js
- styles.css

No Supabase SQL is required.

1. Intelligent Reports:
- Future dates are disabled in the Report Date calendar.
- A future date typed or pasted manually is rejected.
- The date is reset to today's Indian date.
- Patient and Daily Operations reports cannot be generated for a future date.
- WhatsApp sharing therefore cannot use a future report date.

2. Patient Edit confirmation:
- Successful update: fixed green confirmation pop-up.
- Failed update: fixed red error pop-up.
- The notification appears at the top-centre of the visible screen.
- No scrolling is required.
- It closes automatically after 4.5 seconds or can be closed manually.

After uploading:
1. Close all ERP windows.
2. Reopen the app.
3. Tap “App update available” if shown.
4. Confirm Version 1.3.3.
