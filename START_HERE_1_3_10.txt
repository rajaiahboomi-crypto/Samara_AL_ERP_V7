SAMARA CARE ERP 1.3.10 — PHYSIOTHERAPY SESSION ENTRY

1. Run once in Supabase SQL Editor:
   36_physiotherapy_session_workflow.sql

2. Upload and replace only:
   app.js
   index.html
   service-worker.js

New workflow:
- Preferred Time is shown in AM/PM format.
- Time selection uses an easy AM/PM dropdown.
- Admin, Manager, Nurse and Caregiver can record physiotherapy sessions.
- Accounts and Kitchen can view only.
- Status options:
  • Completed
  • Pending
  • Not Done
- Reason is mandatory for Pending and Not Done.
- Optional session notes capture exercises, tolerance, pain and response.
- Recent Physiotherapy Sessions are displayed below the plan.
- Green/red fixed confirmation pop-ups are included.
- Session activity is added to Audit Trail without blocking the clinical save.

No other module is changed.
