SAMARA CARE ERP 1.3.31 — ROOMS, TARIFF & PATIENT TRANSFER MANAGEMENT

   49_rooms_tariff_transfer_management.sql

2. Upload and replace only:
   app.js

MENU
- Added Admin → Rooms.
- Removed Rooms & Beds from Operations.
- Removed room-management access from Accounts.
- Admin and Manager may manage rooms and tariffs.

ROOM MANAGEMENT
- Room/bed master.
- Room type, floor, wing and status.
- Room Rent per day.
- Nursing Charge per day.
- Special Nurse Charge per day.
- Occupancy and availability summary.

ADMISSION CONTROL
- Only Admin or Manager can allot the room and complete admission.
- Admission dropdown displays available room/bed master entries only.
- Availability is rechecked immediately before saving.
- Atomic room assignment prevents double allotment.

ROOM SHIFTING
- Admin/Manager can shift an active patient to another available room.
- Mandatory reason and effective date/time.
- Previous room becomes Available.
- New room becomes Occupied.
- Complete previous-room and new-room history is preserved.
- Patient ID remains unchanged.
- All past bills, payments, advances and charges remain attached to the patient.
- Today's automatic accommodation charges are recalculated using the new tariff.
- Future room, nursing and special-nurse charges use the new room tariff.

DEFAULT TARIFFS
- Private/Single/Deluxe: Room ₹3,000; Nursing ₹1,000.
- Twin/Sharing: Room ₹2,000; Nursing ₹800.
- General/Ward: Room ₹1,800; Nursing ₹750.
- Special Nurse tariff is fixed separately for each room/bed.
