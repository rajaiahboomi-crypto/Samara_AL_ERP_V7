SAMARA CARE ERP 1.2.9 — MANUAL WHATSAPP INTELLIGENT REPORT SHARING

1. Run once in Supabase SQL Editor:
   31_patient_report_whatsapp_history.sql

2. Replace only these files in the GitHub repository root:
   app.js
   index.html
   service-worker.js

3. Close and reopen the ERP. Tap “App update available” if displayed.

How to use:
- Open Intelligent Reports.
- Select a patient and generate the Patient Report.
- For a full report, click Print / Save PDF and save it first.
- Click WhatsApp.
- Choose Patient, Relative or Both.
- Choose Quick Health Update or Full Intelligent Report.
- Choose English or Tamil.
- Click Open WhatsApp.
- For Full Intelligent Report, manually attach the saved PDF before sending.

Important:
The ERP records “WhatsApp Opened” in Communication History. WhatsApp Web/mobile does not confirm that the staff actually pressed Send.
