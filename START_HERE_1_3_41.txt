CORRECTED
The Shift Tasks page is now a worklist and navigation screen only.

The Complete / Exception buttons no longer attempt to save directly from the worklist.
This removes the ON CONFLICT error:
“there is no unique or exclusion constraint matching the ON CONFLICT specification”.

BUTTON BEHAVIOUR

Medicine:
- Complete opens Medicines / Medication Administration.
- The patient, medicine, scheduled time and intended status are preselected.

Basic Care:
- Complete opens the regular Daily Care Entry window.
- Patient, care activity, active shift and Completed status are preselected.
- Exception opens the same regular window with Refused status selected.

Vital Signs:
- Enter Vitals continues to open the regular Vital Signs window.

Physiotherapy:
- Complete opens the regular Physiotherapy Session window.
- Patient, plan and Completed status are preselected.
- Postpone opens the same window with Pending status selected.

The Nurse/Caregiver reviews the regular form and then saves the clinical entry there.
This preserves the normal validation, remarks, audit trail and confirmation process.
