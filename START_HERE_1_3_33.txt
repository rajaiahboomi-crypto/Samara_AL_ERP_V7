SAMARA CARE ERP 1.3.33 — ROOM STATUS & OCCUPANT DROPDOWN

Upload and replace only:
- app.js
- index.html
- service-worker.js

No Supabase SQL is required.

Admission room dropdown now shows:
- Available rooms in Green and selectable.
- Occupied rooms in Red and disabled.
- Reserved rooms in Blue and disabled.
- Maintenance rooms in Grey and disabled.
- Occupied room includes the resident name and Patient ID.
- Room type and tariff remain visible in the same compact line.

Example:
Room 102-A · Twin Sharing · Occupied · Occupied by Mrs. Radhamani (PAT-0004) · Room ₹2,000 + Nursing ₹800/day

Only Available rooms can be allotted during admission.
Occupied, Reserved and Maintenance rooms are shown only for Admin/Manager information.
