SAMARA CARE ERP 1.3.25 — BILL & CHARGE ACTION / DECISION STATUS

1. Run once in Supabase SQL Editor:
   45_bill_charge_action_decision_status.sql

2. Upload and replace only:
   app.js
   index.html
   service-worker.js

Action / Decision now clearly shows:
- Pending
- Approved
- Partially Approved
- Rejected

For Nurse:
- The final action is visible in the Action / Decision column.
- Pending requests can still be edited.
- Approved, partially approved and rejected requests are view-only.

For Admin / Manager / Accounts:
- Approve — posts the full approved amount.
- Partially Approve — asks for the approved amount and mandatory remarks.
- Reject — asks for the rejection reason.
- Each final decision is protected against duplicate posting.

Colour coding:
- Approved: Green
- Partially Approved: Amber
- Rejected: Red
- Pending: Grey

The approved or partially approved amount is automatically posted to Billing & Payments.
