SAMARA CARE ERP 1.3.18 — AUTOMATIC DAILY ROOM & NURSING CHARGES

1. Run once in Supabase SQL Editor:
   42_automatic_daily_room_nursing_charges.sql

2. Upload and replace only:
   app.js
   index.html
   service-worker.js

Fixed daily rates:
SINGLE / SEPARATE ROOM
- Room rent: ₹3,000 per patient per day
- Nursing charge: ₹1,000 per patient per day

TWIN SHARING
- Room rent: ₹2,000 per patient per day
- Nursing charge: ₹800 per patient per day

GENERAL ROOM / WARD
- Room rent: ₹1,800 per patient per day
- Nursing charge: ₹750 per patient per day

How it works:
- When Admin, Manager or Accounts opens Billing & Payments, the ERP verifies today's charges.
- Missing room and nursing charges are entered automatically.
- “Generate / Verify Today” can be used manually.
- Each patient receives only one Room Charge and one Nursing Charge per date.
- Refreshing or reopening cannot create duplicate charges.
- Charges appear directly in the Patient Ledger as “System generated”.
- Discharged/inactive patients and patients without an occupied bed are not charged.
- Future-date generation is blocked.

Room type matching:
- Single, Separate or Private → Single/Separate rate
- General, Ward or Dormitory → General rate
- All other sharing types → Twin Sharing rate
