SAMARA CARE ERP 1.3.19 — BILLING VIEW, DISCREPANCY, ADVANCE & HISTORY

1. Run once in Supabase SQL Editor:
   43_billing_view_discrepancy_advance_history.sql

2. Upload and replace only:
   app.js
   index.html
   service-worker.js

Access:
- Nurse and Caregiver: view bills, charges, payments, advances and history; raise discrepancy queries.
- Accounts/Admin/Manager: full review, correction, closure and transaction entry.
- Nurse keeps the separate Raise Bills & Charges workflow.
- Caregiver cannot create charges but may question an incorrect charge.

Single-click views:
- Pending Bills as on Date
- Complete Transaction History
- Advances / Payments

Summary:
- Total Charges
- Payments / Advance
- Discounts
- Pending Bills
- Advance Balance
- Open Queries

Discrepancy types:
- Wrong amount
- Duplicate charge
- Wrong patient
- Wrong category
- Service not provided
- Wrong date
- Advance not adjusted
- Other

Accounts/Admin/Manager records the decision and closes or corrects the query.
