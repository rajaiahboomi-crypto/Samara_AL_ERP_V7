SAMARA CARE ERP 1.3.37 — PATIENT-CENTRIC UNIFIED NURSING WORKLIST

Upload and replace only:
- app.js
- index.html
- service-worker.js

No Supabase SQL is required.

SHIFT TASKS REDESIGNED

- One compact expandable card per patient.
- The collapsed card shows:
  Patient name
  Patient ID
  Room/bed
  First one or two pending tasks
  “+ more” count
  Pending and completed counts
- Clicking the patient card expands all work for that patient.

EXPANDED PATIENT VIEW

- Medicines due in the active shift.
- Basic care tasks for the active shift.
- Vital signs status.
- Physiotherapy due in the active shift.
- Next-shift care duties in one compact summary.
- Individual Complete, Exception, Given, Postpone and Enter Vitals actions.

SPECIAL NURSE

- Special Nurse information appears once in the patient card.
- It no longer appears against every task.
- The screen clearly states that authorised Nurses and Caregivers may also perform the care tasks.

TOP SUMMARY

- Patients in worklist.
- Patients needing attention.
- Current-shift tasks pending.
- Medicines due.
- Care pending.
- Vitals pending.
- Physiotherapy pending.
- Next-shift care scheduled.

This layout remains practical for 25, 50 or more patients because only one compact row is shown per patient until expanded.
