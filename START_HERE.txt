SAMARA CARE ERP 1.2.1 – MAR ENTRY

Upload and replace only these two files in the GitHub repository root:
1. app.js
2. service-worker.js

No Supabase SQL is required.

After GitHub publishes:
1. Close all Samara Care ERP windows.
2. Reopen the ERP.
3. Tap “App update available” if shown.
4. Confirm version 1.2.1.
5. Open Nursing > Medicines.
6. Click “Administer” beside an active prescription or “Record Dose” under Today’s MAR.

The MAR popup records scheduled time, actual/recorded time, status, remarks/reason, and the logged-in staff member.
