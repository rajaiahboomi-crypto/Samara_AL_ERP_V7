SAMARA CARE ERP 1.2.4 – MEDICATION ERROR ANALYSIS

1. In Supabase, open SQL Editor and run:
   29_medication_error_analysis.sql

2. In GitHub, replace only:
   app.js
   service-worker.js
   index.html

3. Close and reopen the ERP. Confirm Version 1.2.4.

4. Admin/Manager: open Manager > Medication Errors.

The module automatically analyses MAR delays, late documentation, missed doses and omissions. Wrong dose and wrong medicine must be reported by staff because software cannot reliably infer them without a report. The management report includes AI-assisted narrative analysis, filters, review notes, and Print/Save PDF.
