SAMARA CARE ERP 1.3.14 — SPECIAL NURSE ROLE VALIDATION

   39_special_nurse_role_validation.sql

2. Upload and replace only:
   app.js

Internal assignment:
- The dropdown now displays only active employees whose role is Nurse or Caregiver.
- Admin, Manager, Accounts, Kitchen and other roles are excluded.
- Employee ID and role are shown in the dropdown.
- A strict validation prevents an invalid role from being saved even if the form is manipulated.

Outsourced assignment:
- Outsourced nurse name
- Company / Organisation name
- Optional Nursing Council Registration Number
- Organisation contact person and number
- Agreement / Work Order reference

All earlier Special Nurse assignment fields, access permissions, status updates,
Audit Trail and confirmation pop-ups remain unchanged.
