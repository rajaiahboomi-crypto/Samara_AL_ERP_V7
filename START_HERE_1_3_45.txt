SAMARA CARE ERP 1.3.45 — SHIFT TASK COMPLETION SYNCHRONISATION

Upload these four files:
- app.js
- index.html
- service-worker.js
- styles-1.3.45.css

No Supabase SQL is required.

ISSUE FIXED
A Daily Care entry saved successfully, but Shift Tasks continued to show it as pending.

CAUSE
The regular Daily Care form saved patient, date, shift and remarks, but it did not save the original care_order_id from the selected Shift Task. Shift Tasks therefore could not match the saved record to the care-plan task.

CORRECTIONS
- care_order_id now travels from Shift Tasks to Daily Care.
- Daily Care stores care_order_id in care_logs.
- After save, the system returns to Shift Tasks.
- Shift Tasks reloads and marks that exact task Completed / Refused / Not required.
- Pending counts reduce immediately.
- Completed counts increase immediately.
- Historical records without care_order_id are also matched by patient, shift and care-activity text where possible.
- The Daily Care form shows “Linked to the selected Shift Task” when opened from the worklist.

Direct Daily Care entry from the left menu remains available and is not forced to a care order.
